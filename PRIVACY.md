# Hollow Knight Guide — Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 6 November 2026**

Hollow Knight Guide is an independently developed, unofficial fan companion to Hollow Knight and Hollow Knight: Silksong. Support contact: **apolohcr2@outlook.com**. This policy applies to the app's current functionality; it must be reviewed alongside the app store privacy disclosures before public release.

## Information saved on your device

The app uses local device storage for settings, saved charm/tool loadouts, bosses defeated, items collected, quests completed and notes. Photo journal pictures are also saved locally. You do not need to create an account to use these guide features.

You can manually export a backup of guide progress, settings, notes and loadouts from Settings; journal photos are not included. You decide where to save or share exported backups using your device's sharing controls.

## Network requests and external services

The app can request guide updates from its public static API and can load images or pages from community wiki sites. The servers used for those requests may receive standard connection information such as IP address, requested files and device/network details.

Where ads are enabled, the app uses **Google Mobile Ads** and Google's consent interface. Ads may involve device and advertising-related information as described by Google. Where Premium purchases are enabled, the app uses **RevenueCat** to obtain offerings and check purchase entitlements; purchase transactions are processed by the device's app store. RevenueCat may process purchase and device identifiers as described in its privacy notice. These third parties are independent providers:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Permissions and choices

If you choose photo or journal features, the app may ask for camera, photo library, or file permissions. You can change permissions in the device settings. You can reset guide progress and settings in the app, remove journal photos, and manage ad privacy options in Settings where required. Resetting data on your device does not automatically delete information retained by third-party services. If you export a backup, the recipient app or storage service's practices apply.
